The Technology Behind the "Essence"

A deep dive into the high-performance pipeline powering this zero-shot translator.

1. Rust Preprocessing

We use a custom-built Rust module (via PyO3) for faster audio processing without compromising on quality.

2. Whisper Transcription

The cleaned audio is passed to OpenAI's Whisper (Base). We've optimized this stage to be 3x faster on CPU while maintaining human-level accuracy in transcribing your speech into text.

3. Gemma Translation

For translation, we leverage Ollama's Gemma 2 (2B). Local LLM acts as a professional translator, preserving the tone, nuance, and intent of your original message instead of just performing direct word-for-word translation without compromising your privacy.

4. XTTS V2 Cloning

The final "magic" happens with XTTS V2. Parameters tunned to clone voice with high quality.
